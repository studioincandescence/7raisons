01 — The piece

A presence,
not a fixture.

Drawn from lenticular clouds, Les 7 Raisons stacks seven volumes of glass with free, open curves.

The domes graze one another, sometimes overlap, and catch the light the way a weather formation does. At the centre, a single brass axis gathers the seven luminous crowns. The engineering withdraws so that the landscape can appear.

02 — Materials

Glass, self-evident.

Durable, mineral, crossed by light — glass gives each stratum its depth and its permanence.

Thermoforming, casting or hand-shaping: the process will be settled after the first trials with the glassmaker. The mechanical precision of brass answers the subtle irregularity of the material. An optical acrylic version, lighter and more affordable, remains available for the larger scales and for suspended installations.

05 — Scale & value

Every piece is
a proposal.

The budget is built from the scale, the complexity of the glass and the constraints of the installation.

A Gallery composition at Ø 900 mm calls for more material, a reinforced structure and more workshop time than a Collection piece at Ø 600 mm. Until the prototype is validated, we would rather give a fair estimate than an artificially precise price.
