No two are alike

Seven diameters, seven curves, seven places.

Compact

The seven strata stay close to the axis. The cloud holds together, dense, almost solid. For a low ceiling, or a room you look at from close by.

Balanced

The reference. The widest stratum sits fourth, the composition closes above and below. This is the reading closest to a lenticular cloud.

Open

The strata spread and overlap widely. The cloud takes the room, the light carries further. For tall volumes.
